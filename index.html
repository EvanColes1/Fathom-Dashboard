                                                          About Fathom

                                                          Method

                                                          How Fathom collects, tags and scores the data.

                                                          Collection

                                                          Every three hours the collector reads 35 news and research feeds, trying a backup address if a publisher blocks the main one. It removes duplicate stories by link and by headline, and keeps 14 days of history. Sanctions and energy data refresh once a day.

                                                          Home Desk

                                                          Once a day the Home Desk reads the latest GOV.UK publications from eight departments, this year's sanctions and export control laws, and Hansard. For each of the week's most covered countries it counts spoken contributions in Parliament over the last 30 days. Comparing that share with the country's share of global coverage shows where Westminster is ahead of the news, and where it is quiet.

                                                          Tagging

                                                          Each story is matched against more than 250 theme keywords and 500 country, capital and leader names. Headline matches count double. Short acronyms such as US, UK and EU only match in capitals, so the word “us” is never read as the United States.

                                                          Signals

                                                          For every theme, region and country, Fathom compares the last 24 hours with the average day over the previous 13. A signal needs at least 3 stories and is ranked by the ratio (24-hour count + 1) ÷ (baseline + 1), which stops tiny numbers from producing huge ratios.

                                                          Fathom Depth Index

                                                          A 0–100 score for each country, built from four parts. Each part is scored 0 to 1, then weighted:

                                                            Bands: Low under 25, Guarded 25–44, Elevated 45–64, High 65–79, Severe 80 and over.

                                                            The Surface Index, Fathom's original score, is kept for comparison. It reads only how much coverage a country gets. Soundings break each country into six areas, Swell warnings flag scores climbing fast for three days, Currents group the same story from many outlets into one event, and the Logbook tests ratings against what happened next.

                                                            Confidence is High when at least 10 independent sources cover the country and a month of baseline exists, Medium with fewer sources or a shorter baseline, and Low otherwise. Fathom stores a daily history of up to 120 days, so baselines strengthen over time. The index measures risk signals in open sources. It is not a forecast.
